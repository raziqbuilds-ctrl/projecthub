Rule 1

A student cannot apply to their own project.

Rule 2

A student cannot apply twice to the same project.

Rule 3

A student cannot apply if the project is no longer recruiting.

Rule 4

A student cannot be accepted if the team is already full.

Rule 5

Once accepted, the student becomes a team member.

Rule 6

A student cannot have duplicate membership in the same team.

Rule 7

The project owner cannot accept more people than the maximum team size.

These are the kinds of rules that make the project more than basic CRUD.
